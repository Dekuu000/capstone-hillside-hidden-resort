1.5 Scope and Limitation of the Study
The project aims to develop an Autonomous Home Security Patrol Robot designed to enhance surveillance for residential properties in Olongapo City. The scope encompasses the integration of advanced features such as motion detection, real-time alert notifications, camera systems for video monitoring, and a buzzer alarm to deter intruders. The robot will operate within a predefined patrol area, providing security solutions tailored to residential needs.
However, the study is limited by several factors. Environmental conditions, such as weather and obstacles, may affect the robot's functionality and efficiency. Additionally, constraints related to Arduino's processing capabilities and battery life could impact the implementation of complex features. The patrol area is also restricted to a specific perimeter, limiting its effectiveness for larger properties. Finally, potential false alerts from pets or moving objects may require further refinement of the detection algorithms to enhance reliability.

### [Heading 3] Scope of the Study
Objective: The project aims to develop an Autonomous Home Security Patrol Robot utilizing Arduino technology for smart surveillance.
Features:
Motion Detection: The robot will use sensors to detect movement within its patrol area.
Real-Time Alerts: Notifications will be sent to the homeowner's device in case of suspicious activity.
Camera Integration: A camera system will provide video surveillance and monitoring capabilities.
Buzzer Alarm: An alarm will be triggered in case of unauthorized entry or unusual activity.
Target Users: This study focuses on residential properties in Olongapo City that require enhanced security measures.

### [Heading 3] Limitations of the Study
Environmental Factors: The robot's effectiveness may be impacted by weather conditions (rain, wind) or obstacles in the environment.
Technology Constraints: Limitations related to Arduino processing power and battery life may restrict advanced features or long operational times.
Range of Operation: The patrol area will be limited to a predefined perimeter, affecting its ability to monitor larger properties.
Camera Limitations: The quality of video surveillance may vary based on lighting conditions and camera specifications.
False Alerts: The system may generate false alarms due to pets, moving objects, or environmental changes, requiring further refinement.

1.6 Definition of Terms
Autonomous Home Security Patrol Robot: A self-operating robotic device designed to monitor and secure residential properties through automated patrol routes and surveillance capabilities.
Motion Detection: A technology that identifies and responds to movement within a specified area, enabling the robot to detect potential intruders or unusual activity.
Real-Time Alerts: Immediate notifications sent to homeowners' devices, such as smartphones or computers, informing them of detected movements or security breaches.
Camera Integration: The incorporation of a camera system into the robot to provide visual surveillance and recording capabilities, enabling homeowners to monitor their property remotely.
Buzzer Alarm: A sound-emitting device on the robot that activates in response to detected intrusions or emergencies, serving as a deterrent to potential threats.
Arduino: An open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software, widely used in the development of interactive projects like this security robot.
Obstacle Avoidance: The capability of the robot to detect and navigate around obstacles in its environment, ensuring safe and efficient patrols.
